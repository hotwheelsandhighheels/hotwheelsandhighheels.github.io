    <h2>About me</h2>
    <p>I had already written an about me but my best friend told me it wasn't enough, so here is this page.</p>
    <p>I grew up in San Diego, between Encanto and Grant Hill. I went to church in Logan Heights. I went to elementary school in Golden Hill. Pretty much, I know San Diego well and I love it here. </p>
    <p>I'm from a big Mexican family. We love spending time together. Sometimes we will plan a party just to see each other and spend all day together. It's always been clear to me that I am beyond blessed and privileged to have such a beautiful family.</p>
    <p>My grandmother used to say "Amar es entregarse." To love is to give yourself over completely.</p>
    <p>This project is a combination of some many years of thinking and reading and writing and having unorganized notes and unarticulated feelings. I think I drove several professors crazy trying to explain myself and in turn felt frustrated with the pessimism of an older generation who just didn't get it.</p>
    <p>Millennials have always been a special interest of mine, not only because I am one, but because in 2013, when I was 23 years old, Time Magazine released an article titled, "Me Me Me."</p>
    <p>For over a decade, Millennials have been the focal point of social commentary. We have affected politics, art, the economy, culture -- and they won't let us forget it.</p>
    <p>Generations older group anyone younger under the label. Generations younger than us reject the label. The difference between the two is that younger generations are experiencing the world alongside Millennials.</p>
    <p>In grad school, I attempted to write a poetry manuscript filled with love poems to a generation. I dove into archival work that blurred the lines between memory and technology. And now I am doing this, an archive of the Millennial Epoch.</p>
    <p>I don't really know what to say when people ask why I'm doing this or what for. People always want an end goal and all I want is to have fun.</p>
    <p>Memory work is the most fun I've had. History, poetry, art, documentaries, technology. The things that make humans human. Yeah.</p>
