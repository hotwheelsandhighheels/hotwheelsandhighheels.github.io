    <h2>About me</h2>
<p>Hey!</p>
<p>You found my secret about me page that is a lil more detailed and about ME than the other page.</p>
<p>A few things about me that only my family and close friends tend to notice:</p>
<p>I love to sing and especially singing for others. I like to sing songs from Selena or Juan Gabriel. Growing up, I was part of different choirs. My favorite was singing to a church full of family on Sunday mornings. I like to think that if my family had stayed together how we were, I might still be religious. Something about community and fitting in.</p>
<p>I like to clean. I am always cleaning. And organizing and decluttering.</p>
<p>If too much mess piles up, I'll start doing dishes. I love washing dishes. I love the dishwasher too tho.</p>
<p>My favorite part of cleaning is getting the dirt off and seeing it on the rag. Or seeing how dirty the mop water gets. It's highkey satisfying for me, but I know some people don't like it.</p>
<p>I guess arts & crafts are what I normally get into. I like cross-stitching. My last cross-stich project was the Last Supper tho and that one is so large I only finished like half of it. Ok, maybe a lil more than half. I gotta get all the small bits now. Special details like the eyes and stuff. I like cutting and pasting, and building like making little models of things. I like to color and draw and paint sometimes. I used to be hella into calligraphy when I was younger so I got really good at copying. I wouldn't say I can draw at all lmao, but I can copy down different lettering. I like graffiti letters best. I also like to make bees-wax food wraps once in a while.</p>
<p>Not that it matters in the grand scheme of things, but I actually like taking care of our planet. I like picking up trash, and separating waste into different categories, compost, recycling, stuff we can reuse or upcycle. I got that from my mama.</p>
<p>I am not an athletic person at all but I love riding my bike and going swimming. My sibling custom built my bike, it's a single-speed road/bmx hybrid, painted copper-gold and black. It's sick. It looks so hood I love it. I wanted a bmx hybrid cause I wanted to do tricks. I gotta learn how to do tricks tho lmao all I can do is a lil bounce here n there.</p>
<p>Recently, I saw that most of my favorite books were under the sociology section at the bookstore. I've always had a particular interest in history and culture, so that makes sense. I always had an interest with my generation (as has the rest of the world), which is how the foundations of this project came to be.</p>
<p>I already said my cousins are my best friends, my first and life-long best friends. But just to give a shout out... my bestie of 10 years, Faith, and I met when we were working together at Charlotte Russe. She was cracking me up at the holiday party. Pretty soon we had a lil momentum going on while working. I seen a set of friendship necklaces said "Girl Gang" and that was the start of our best friendship.</p>
<p>Estrella, my best friend of 23 years, is my favorite person in the whole wide world. And she is mother to another one of my favorite people.</p>
<p>My kid, Stella, is the reason there are seventeen years of social media posting to sift through, and why I am doing this project now. She is archived on Tumblr and Instragram and Youtube. She was on Vine and Tiktok and Facebook. I never posted her for the world to see. I kept what I could private and only visible to people I trusted. It is impossible to dearchive her, and myself, from the web completely. </p>
<p>There is so much that goes into this passion project of mine & I hope it becomes clear as you read through the pages. I can't possibly articulate it all in a few short sentences. I hope you like it :) </p>
<p>Love, <br> Ale 💕</p>
