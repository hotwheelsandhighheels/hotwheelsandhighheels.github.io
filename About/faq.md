# the Manual

## Take the Wheel

### Drivers

I publish one feature a month. Usually, I will reach out to those I want to interview. However, I am also taking requests. Ask me to interview someone by filling out this [form.](/blessings/) If *you* would like to be interviewed, fill out and email me the Driver's Survey. 

### Secret Surveys

There are throwback Myspace bulletin board surveys scattered through out the site. Submitted responses are considered for publication.

## Trunk Content

### Provenance 

I try my best to ask permission from and credit the original creator for their content. That said, with the nature of the web, it is not always possible to trace provenance that far. 

The artifacts I choose to publish are found online, presumably posted for public consumption. They are tagged with the most authentic information I could find. 

### Revisions

All content in the *Trunk* is open to suggestions and/or requests for revisions or alterations. Please email me for further discussion.
